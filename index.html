A note from Sumit, founder of Rareearth Wellness

Breathing changed how I felt every single day.

For eight or nine years of my corporate life, I started each day with two simple practices: Anulom Vilom and Kapalbhati. What surprised me was how quickly I felt it. Not weeks later, but the same day. I'd finish and feel happy, curious and full of energy, and that feeling stayed with me all day.

Later I began simply sitting and watching my breath, the first step into dhyan. Today that quiet time is the most rewarding part of my day.

I made Breath Lab so anyone, at any age, can try the same simple steps, explained gently and completely free.

Rooted in our own stories

Many of us grew up hearing our grandmothers' stories from the Ramayana and the Mahabharata: Hanuman's strength, Parashurama's courage, Nakula's speed. So many of these heroes spent time in dhyan, and dhyan always begins in the same place: watching the breath.

In the Gita, Krishna asks Arjuna to sit straight and steady, with the breath balanced. Breath Lab brings that timeless practice into a modern day.

Your 15-minute invitation

If this sparks even an iota of curiosity, give Breath Lab just fifteen minutes. If you feel a real, noticeable difference, come back tomorrow. If you don't, that's perfectly fine. You've lost nothing.
