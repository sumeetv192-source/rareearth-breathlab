Can it replace my teen's glasses?

No. Drishtisheel nourishes the eyes with vitamins A and C and antioxidants, but it doesn't correct eyesight. Keep up regular eye check-ups.

How does it taste?

Bright and tangy from amla, slightly sweet from carrot and mishri, with a fresh saunf finish.

Can younger children take it?

It's made from everyday foods. For children under 10, use half the serving.

What else helps tired eyes?

The 20-20-20 rule, blinking breaks, good lighting, outdoor play and enough sleep. Our free Breath Lab also has eye-relaxing breathing breaks.

Why is it made only after I order?
