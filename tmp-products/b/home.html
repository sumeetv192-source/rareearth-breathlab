Questions parents ask

Good to know

Who is Rareearth for?

Parents of teenagers (roughly 12–19) who want simple, natural daily habits for their children's body, breath and mind.

Do I need to use all three pillars?

No. Start with whatever your teen needs most – a kadha, a free Breath Lab exercise or Inner Compass. They work best together, but each stands on its own.

Are the kadhas medicines?

No. They are food products made from everyday kitchen spices and herbs, traditionally used in Indian homes. Benefits described relate to the ingredients. Please consult a doctor for any medical condition.

How fast do kadhas arrive?

We make your pouch fresh after you order and dispatch within 48 hours. Delivery usually takes 3–7 working days.

Is Breath Lab really free?

Yes – the starter exercises are free. A guided 7-day course with longer daily sessions costs ₹50.
